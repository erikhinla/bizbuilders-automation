Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the procedure take? +
The initial procedure typically takes 2-3 hours, including consultation, design, and application.
How long do the results last? +
Results typically last 2-3 years, depending on your skin type and lifestyle. Touch-ups are recommended annually.
Does it hurt? +
Most clients experience minimal discomfort. We use topical numbing creams to ensure your comfort throughout the procedure.
What's the healing process like? +
Healing takes about 10-14 days. Your brows will initially appear darker, then lighten as they heal to the perfect shade.
